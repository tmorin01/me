Back in April, we had some prospective students visiting as part of my program's [Diversity Travel Fellowship Program](https://www.bu.edu/neuro/academics/graduate/graduate-program-for-neuroscience-diversity-travel-fellowship-program/). During their visit, the prospective students had lunch with current graduate students studying neuroscience, toured a few labs, and did some sightseeing around Boston. In the afternoon, I helped conduct a tour of the [Cognitive Neuroimaging Center](http://www.bu.edu/neuroimaging/) and we scanned a pineapple together! Here I've uploaded interactive versions of two different scans from that day:
